# SOP-011 — Daily Reports & Project Photography
Owner: Shawqi (Construction PM / Field Lead) | Status: Draft | Priority: High

## Problem this fixes
Shawqi is alone in the field with no backup and nothing captured day to day — no record if a dispute, delay, or safety issue comes up later.

## Rule
Every active-construction project gets one dated field entry per working day. No exceptions for "nothing happened" days — log that too (2 lines is enough).

## Procedure
1. **End of each field day**, Shawqi sends a short raw note (voice memo, text, or bullet points is fine) covering: weather, crew/equipment on site, work performed, any issues or delays, photos taken.
2. **A-05 (Construction/Field agent, Pilot)** drafts a structured daily report from that raw note into `projects/<ID>/.../daily-reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — this is a draft only.
3. **Shawqi reviews and approves** the draft before it's final — correct anything wrong, confirm anything ambiguous. Nothing goes in the record unreviewed.
4. Photos get saved into the project's field-photos folder, named `<ID>_YYYY-MM-DD_##`.

## Who owns what
- Sending the raw note: Shawqi, daily, no exceptions
- Drafting the structured report: A-05 (agent, supervised)
- Approving/correcting before it's final: Shawqi only

## Exit criteria for this SOP
- 10 consecutive working days logged with no gaps, each reviewed and approved by Shawqi before being marked final.
